# Forklift boost wind

Source: Vefects, **Wind VFX for Godot**, v1.0.3.

- Official page: https://store.godotengine.org/asset/vefects/wind-vfx/

- Original `LICENSE.txt` is included verbatim. Free for commercial finished games; do not redistribute these assets or derivatives as standalone files, asset packs or in public repositories.

The three TGA files are unmodified originals from `Vefects/Wind VFX/VFX/Textures/`. Their PNG counterparts are lossless format conversions, verified pixel-for-pixel, for this engine's import support. The forklift material adapts the selected pack's **Wind Flows 02 Unlit** recipe: green-channel streak mask, blue-channel cutout and green-channel panning noise. It uses an original low-poly shell fitted to the forklift. This is a modified use of Vefects assets, not CC0.
