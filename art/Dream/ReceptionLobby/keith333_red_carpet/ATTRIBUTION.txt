The author states that these textures are based on photographs taken by them,
made seamless and supplied with a generated normal map.

Original files: RedCarpet_S.jpg and RedCarpet_N.jpg, unchanged.
FiveMinuteMore uses the complete photographic colour and matching normal maps
with a darker burgundy material tint, mipmaps and the game's pixel sampling.
The material is applied to new low-poly carpet geometry and folded binding.
No endorsement by the original author is implied.
