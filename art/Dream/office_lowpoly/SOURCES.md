# Office environment assets

The selected GLB models in `kenney/` are from **Kenney Furniture Kit**.

- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/furniture-kit
- Official archive: https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/furniture-kit/440e0608a4-1677580847/kenney_furniture-kit.zip
- License: CC0 1.0; original license included at `kenney/License.txt`.
- Downloaded: 2026-09-21.
- Changes at runtime: normalized floor origin, shared muted rough materials, footprint scaling.

Server racks, copier, partitions, whiteboard and reception counter are project-native procedural geometry. No external texture or paid asset is required.
