# Level 1-1 Atrium Loop CC0 Asset Provenance

All 3D textures, calibrated modular industrial hardware, and shaders used in the rebuilt `1-1` Atrium Loop (suspended L-corridor overlooking open `1F` H06 hangar bay) originate from verified CC0 1.0 Public Domain sources, core *FiveMinuteMore* (`0-1..0-6`) shaders, and the project's H06 Crawler Hero Asset.

## 1. Central Hero Asset
- `res://art/Dream/H06/crawler_h06.glb` — H06 六足战术爬虫机甲 standing openly on the exposed `1F` test plinth (no glass containment enclosure).

## 2. Core Game Surface Shaders & Poly Haven CC0 Textures
License: CC0 1.0 Universal (`https://polyhaven.com/license`)
- Core shaders (`res://shaders/dream3d/atrium_loop_surface.gdshader`, `res://shaders/dream3d/maintenance_concrete.gdshader`, `res://shaders/dream3d/transit_surfaces.gdshader`) calibrated to clean tech-brutalist industrial tones: damp dark charcoal `1F` concrete deck & recessed steel grate pit trimmed in safety yellow, matte off-white/cool-grey suspended corridor floor slabs and precast composite wall panels.
- `res://art/Dream/CC0/AtriumLoop11/polyhaven/precast_concrete_wall` (`_diff_1k.jpg`, `_nor_gl_1k.jpg`, `_rough_1k.jpg`) — Charcoal structural soffits and under-corridor hangar bulkheads.
