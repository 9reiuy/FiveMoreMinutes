# Poly Haven architectural surface maps

All selected maps are CC0 1.0 Universal. Official license: https://polyhaven.com/license

- Concrete Floor Worn 001: https://polyhaven.com/a/concrete_floor_worn_001 — Dimitrios Savva (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing).
- Plastered Wall 05: https://polyhaven.com/a/plastered_wall_05 — Charlotte Baglioni.

These are retained research candidates. They are **not active in 1-1** after the user's style correction: the level now directly reuses the existing 0-1/0-2 concrete and 0-4/0-5 surface finishes. No claim of visual acceptance is made for the rejected photographic treatment.

The original 1K Diffuse, Normal (OpenGL) and Roughness files remain in the acquisition folder. Each `*_SOURCE.json` records the official file URLs and original SHA256 hashes. Game copies are native Godot texture resources, resampled to 128 pixels and repeated over 4 metres, maintaining 32 texels per metre and Nearest sampling. No procedural noise or new bitmap artwork replaces these photographs.

Other candidates downloaded during research (large grey tiles, precast wall) are not used in the level because their moss/brick-like patterns do not match the research facility.
