# Red sea city silhouettes

Building geometry is derived from **Blade runner style Cityscapes** by
**Suraj99**, https://blendswap.com/blend/24947. The source is CC BY. The
project-supplied archive records CC BY 4.0, as documented in
`art/Dream/CityDepthShape/SOURCES.md`.

These seven shapes retain the vertex positions, normals and triangle indices
of items 3, 6, 8, 9, 13, 14 and 16 in `source_city_bank.res`. Their original
materials, textures, UV data and vertex colours are omitted. The three shared
materials and the harbour, middle and far building arrangement were made for
this red sea scene. Source attribution is also stored in each native mesh.
